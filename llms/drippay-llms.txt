# drip

> drip is the CRM that fills itself in: AI conversation automation in a Mac app. drip syncs LinkedIn, iMessage, and email conversations, drafts replies in the user's voice, follows up, and books meetings. It drafts by default with optional autopilot, and messages stay on the user's Mac. drip is free to start, with paid plans at $30 per seat plus usage. Built by Drippay, Inc., a Y Combinator company (YC P26).

## What drip is

- A Mac app that syncs LinkedIn, iMessage, and email conversations into one place.
- The CRM that fills itself in: contacts, threads, and follow-ups stay current without manual data entry.
- AI conversation automation: drip drafts replies in the user's voice, follows up when threads go quiet, and books meetings.
- Drafts by default. Autopilot is optional, so nothing sends automatically unless the user turns it on.
- Local-first: messages stay on the user's Mac.
- Free to start. Paid plans are $30 per seat plus usage.

## Privacy and trust

- drip is local-first: messages stay on the user's Mac.
- drip drafts by default. Nothing sends automatically unless the user enables autopilot for a thread or contact.
- drip does not train a shared AI model on user messages.
- iMessage history is read locally on the Mac only when the user grants the required macOS permissions.

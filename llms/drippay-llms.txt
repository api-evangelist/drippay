# dreach

> dreach is the revenue intelligence platform for staffing firms, in a Mac app. Its Opportunity Radar watches hiring demand, leadership changes, past placements, and company events, then shows staffing teams who to contact, why now, and what to say. It drafts outreach in the user's voice, follows up, and books the meeting. It is not a staffing CRM, ATS, contact database, or system of record. Drafts wait in Review until the user sends them or turns on Autopilot, and messages stay on the user's Mac. dreach offers a seven-day card-required trial; Solo is $29 per month including 10 million AI credits, with additional usage at $3.50 per million credits. Built by Drippay, Inc., a Y Combinator company (YC P26).

## What dreach is

- The revenue intelligence platform for staffing firms: its Opportunity Radar catches the opportunities a team would otherwise miss.
- Every opportunity carries evidence (hiring demand, leadership changes, company events), relationship context (past placements, former candidates), a "why now," and a recommended next move.
- "The CRM that fills itself in" is dreach's tagline: contacts, threads, and follow-ups stay current without manual data entry.
- Not a staffing CRM, ATS, contact database, or system of record. dreach works alongside the system a team chooses.
- dreach drafts outreach in the user's voice, follows up when threads go quiet, and books meetings.
- Drafts wait in Review until the user sends them. Autopilot is the one switch that authorizes automatic sends, follow-ups, and replies.
- Local-first: messages stay on the user's Mac.
- Seven-day card-required trial. Solo is $29 per month including 10 million AI credits, with additional usage at $3.50 per million credits.

## Who dreach is for

Staffing agencies, recruiting agencies, and executive search firms are core dreach use cases for business development, job orders, and winning clients. Each vertical has a dedicated page:

- [AI for staffing agencies](https://dreach.ai/ai-for-staffing-agencies): most AI for staffing works the candidate side (sourcing, screening, texting); dreach is the client side: it finds companies from hiring signals, starts prospect conversations through email or LinkedIn, follows up, and books the intake call.
- [Staffing agency business development](https://dreach.ai/bd-for-staffing-firms): finds client leads and job-order opportunities from buying signals like open roles, ranks the best matches, and works them to a booked intake call.
- [AI tools for recruiting agencies](https://dreach.ai/ai-tools-for-recruiting-agencies): AI tools sorted by desk job (sourcing, outreach, ATS and CRM, contact data, notes), plus the client-side gap dreach covers with outreach, follow-up, and booking.
- [Business development for recruiting agencies](https://dreach.ai/bd-for-recruiting): works hiring signals, opens with candidates worth meeting, messages the managers who own the roles through email or LinkedIn, and books the intro call.
- [AI for executive search](https://dreach.ai/ai-for-executive-search): AI on the origination side, not the assessment side; dreach watches succession triggers, drafts client development outreach in the partner's voice, keeps referral networks warm, and books the pitch.

## Privacy and trust

- dreach is local-first: messages stay on the user's Mac.
- dreach sends on autopilot under rules the user sets: people, channels, and daily limits. Any thread can be held for review.
- dreach does not train a shared AI model on user messages.
- iMessage history is read locally on the Mac only when the user grants the required macOS permissions.
